# Markers need context and ownership

## Terminology

Read the relevant definitions in [GLOSSARY.md](../../GLOSSARY.md) before assessment.

## Core idea

A directional arrow conveys information under a convention; it does not prove that its indicated destination is the team’s usable exit. A nondirectional cookie has no intrinsic arrow direction. Personal or team meaning depends on agreed placement and identification. Regional and site practices vary.

A line marker is the broader category. A REM (referencing exit marker) records a personal or team directional reference; other teams must not treat it as their exit instruction. A cookie has no intrinsic direction, even when its placement carries agreed meaning for its owner. See the glossary for these distinctions and usage variations.

## Original example

An unfamiliar directional arrow points away from the team’s recorded exit direction. Do not reinterpret the team route solely to match the arrow. Resolve navigation against the continuous verified route and trained procedures.
