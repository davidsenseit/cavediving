# Markers need context and ownership

## Core idea

A directional marker conveys information under a convention; it does not prove that its indicated destination is the team’s usable exit. A nondirectional cookie has no intrinsic arrow direction. Personal or team meaning depends on agreed placement and identification. Regional and site practices vary.

## Original example

An unfamiliar arrow points away from the team’s recorded exit direction. Do not reinterpret the team route solely to match the arrow. Resolve navigation against the continuous verified route and trained procedures.
