# A dive computer models decompression, not individual safety

## Core idea

A dive computer uses recorded depth, time, configured gases and model settings to calculate decompression guidance. Its displayed limits are not measurements of the diver's actual tissue state or a personal probability of decompression sickness. Remaining within a displayed limit does not eliminate decompression sickness risk. Correct gas settings and device use matter. A symptom concern deserves appropriate medical assessment even when the log appears compliant.

## Original example

Two divers complete a similar profile; one feels unwell. Matching displays do not rule out a medical problem.
