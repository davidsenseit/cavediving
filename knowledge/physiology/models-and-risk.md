# A decompression model estimates risk

## Core idea

A dive computer models exposure; it does not measure all of a diver’s physiology. Remaining within a displayed limit does not eliminate decompression sickness risk. Depth-time history, gas, settings and correct device use matter. A symptom concern deserves appropriate medical assessment even when the log appears compliant.

## Original example

Two divers complete a similar profile; one feels unwell. Matching displays do not rule out a medical problem.
