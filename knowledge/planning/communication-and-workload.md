# A message needs shared meaning

## Core idea

A gesture is useful only if the receiver can perceive and interpret it. Confirm the team’s hand, light and tactile communication within its training. Added tasks consume attention; a survey or camera objective must not displace awareness of gas, route, teammates or position.

## Original example

A photographer repeatedly misses a teammate’s communication. Treat this as a workload and communication problem, not proof that the message was understood.
