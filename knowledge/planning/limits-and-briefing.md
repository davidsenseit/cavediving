# A plan needs limits and responses

## Core idea

An objective is not a plan. Record the intended route and verified exit, team capabilities, gas assumptions, time and depth constraints, equipment checks, communication methods, environmental uncertainties and responses to foreseeable failures. Agree how any diver ends the dive; destination pressure must not override a limit.

## Original example

A team wants to reach a chamber but cannot agree what a turn signal means. Resolve that disagreement before entering, rather than expecting underwater negotiation to fix it.
