# Control position before adding tasks

## Core idea

Buoyancy concerns vertical balance; trim concerns body orientation. A diver can hold depth while directing fin wash into sediment. Propulsion must suit the available space and substrate; no named kick is universally suitable. Practice position control and task loading under appropriate supervision.

## Original example

A diver holds depth during a camera task but repeatedly clouds the floor. A stable depth trace alone misses the propulsion and trim problem.

## Distinguish the observations

- Depth behavior shows whether vertical position is being maintained.
- Body orientation and fin clearance help assess trim in the available space.
- Sediment movement helps assess the effect of propulsion and other movement.

These observations answer different questions. A diver can maintain depth with poor orientation, or disturb sediment while turning during a task. Adding buoyant lift is not automatically a correction for either problem. Choose supervised practice that identifies the cause before adding complexity.
