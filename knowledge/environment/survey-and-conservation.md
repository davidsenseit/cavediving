# A survey is evidence with limits

## Core idea

A map represents recorded observations, not a guarantee of present access or a navigable route. Preserve station identity, measurement units, date, method and uncertainty when discussing survey data. Permission to dive does not automatically include permission to modify lines, collect samples or publish sensitive locations. Protect fragile formations and habitats.

## Original example

A proposed survey adds work close to delicate sediment. Assess competence, permission, task loading and environmental effects before adding it to the dive.
