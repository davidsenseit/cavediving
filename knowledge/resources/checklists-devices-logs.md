# A packing list is not a functional check

## Core idea

Keep packing completeness, functional readiness and team planning distinct. Owning an item does not establish charge, operation or accessibility. Record computer model, firmware, mode and manual revision before interpreting settings or logs. A log can document measurements; it cannot certify route continuity, low-impact propulsion or team competence.

## Original example

A recreational-mode manual corresponding to firmware V91 and release notes for another version are available. Neither establishes the device’s currently installed firmware or supplies the missing technical-mode guidance.

Explain the assumption most likely to make the example unsafe if ignored.

## Learning objective
Explain what each check establishes and what evidence remains missing.

## Packing, function and team use
A packing list confirms presence. A function check addresses operation under the check conditions. A team drill addresses access and interaction in the tested configuration. None alone demonstrates all three.

For each checklist item ask: what is being checked, what failure could be detected, and what remains untested? For example, an operating light may still have insufficient duration; a regulator that supplies gas may still be difficult to donate in the actual configuration.

## Use the debrief
Separate measurements from observations and interpretations. A depth record shows vertical history, while teammate observations may reveal orientation, sediment disturbance or missed messages. Record the specific uncertainty and the next practice objective rather than labelling a whole dive simply good or bad.
