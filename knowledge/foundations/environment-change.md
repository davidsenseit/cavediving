# Conditions change the return problem

## Core idea

Flow, visibility and passage geometry affect both entry and exit. An easy inward swim does not establish an easy return. Clear water at entry does not guarantee visibility later. Record the uncertainty in the plan rather than treating a previous visit as current evidence.

## Original example

A team has only last season’s description of a passage. Its first decision is whether current information is sufficient, not how to reach the previous distance.
