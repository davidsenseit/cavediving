# Understand sixths and thirds allocations

## Prerequisites

GAS-004 and GAS-005: inventories and conversions.

## Learning objective

Apply a stated entry fraction and explain both the entry allowance and what must remain at the nominal turn.

## Establish the applicable restriction first

The rule of sixths permits at most one sixth of the specified starting inventory for entry; five sixths remains at the nominal turn. The rule of thirds permits at most one third for entry; two thirds remains at the nominal turn. The thirds model conventionally distinguishes entry, ordinary return and contingency portions. Under a sixths entry restriction, do not reinterpret all five remaining sixths as discretionary search gas.

The applicable training, configuration, route and conditions determine the permitted allocation. These exercises explicitly supply it. More restrictive entry allowances may be required; do not choose thirds because a longer penetration is wanted. This lesson does not establish certification privileges or a new restriction for a particular course level. Confirm the applicable restriction before an actual plan.

A fraction divides inventory, not time or distance. It does not measure the gas actually needed for an emergency exit. The team can need an earlier turn because of other limits or insufficient contingency supply.

## Worked comparison

Use 24 L at 240 bar: starting quantity is 5,760 reference L.

| Allocation | Maximum entry use | Nominal turn pressure | Quantity remaining |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sixths | 40 bar = 960 reference L | 200 bar | 4,800 reference L |
| Thirds | 80 bar = 1,920 reference L | 160 bar | 3,840 reference L |

The remaining pressure is found by subtraction from the actual start. At either turn, 'remaining' includes the gas needed for leaving; it is not all spare gas.

## Guided calculation

For 20 L at 210 bar, separately calculate a stipulated sixths allowance and a stipulated thirds allowance. Give entry use and remaining gas in both units. Do not infer that either allocation suits a real dive.

## Guided answer — reveal after the learner attempts

Starting quantity is 4,200 reference L. Sixths: entry 35 bar = 700 reference L; turn 175 bar = 3,500 reference L remaining. Thirds: entry 70 bar = 1,400 reference L; turn 140 bar = 2,800 reference L remaining.
