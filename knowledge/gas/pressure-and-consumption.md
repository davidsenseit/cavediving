# Use absolute pressure and explicit units

## Core idea

For an ideal-gas approximation at comparable temperature, cylinder water volume in litres multiplied by pressure drop in bar approximates consumed surface-equivalent litres when referenced to 1 bar. Surface-normalized breathing volume rate is that quantity divided by time and time-weighted mean absolute pressure expressed as a ratio to the reference surface pressure. A bar/min pressure rate depends on cylinder size; it is not interchangeable with L/min.

## Original example

With 18 L water volume, 50 bar used in 15 min at a constant 3 bar absolute and a 1 bar reference: 18 × 50 / (15 × 3) = 20 L/min. This is a classroom idealization, not a future emergency consumption rate.
