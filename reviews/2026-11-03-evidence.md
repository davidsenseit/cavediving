## Supplied source handling

- PSAI cave diving manual: inspected topic inventory to identify omissions. No chapter-by-chapter rewrite, passages, tables, diagrams, qualification claims or exams incorporated. Its older medical and equipment material is not treated as current authority.
- Navigation supplement: inspected as context, identified as Gibb's article and located the independent online original. Local procedures were not converted into universal rules.
- Separate gas-matching PDF: arithmetic recomputed in the calculation review; not accepted as an operational model.
- Cave checklist.xlsx: inspected as an equipment packing inventory, not a validated pre-dive safety checklist. No workbook uploaded to the repository. The worksheet in learning/functional-review.md uses independently selected evidence categories.
- Perdix documents: inspected for scope/version boundaries only. No menu sequences, screenshots or manufacturer text reproduced.
- PSAI_Full_Cave_Quiz.md: deliberately not used to author or seed questions. New scenarios and distractors were written from the concept inventory rather than the supplied exam.

## Conflicts and pending checks

Regional navigation conventions and team configurations cannot be collapsed into one operating protocol. Follow the project’s separate navigation and gas references when discussing those sources, but expose contradictions and arithmetic defects rather than silently endorsing them. Generic entries here do not replace those source-specific procedures. No current Cueva del Agua conditions were checked in this development task.

CDG's 2024 risk assessment appeared in search results but direct retrieval failed (404); it is not recorded as a fully checked source. An expert should especially review reserve-model assumptions, terminology, communication scenarios, common-mode failures and physiology before any status promotion.
