## Editorial scope

The curriculum uses an independent concept structure, original scenarios and explicitly stated mathematical assumptions. Public references support individual factual claims; they are not templates for prose, exercises or illustrations. No third-party training text, examination or image is included.

## Conflicts and pending checks

Regional navigation conventions and team configurations cannot be collapsed into one operating protocol. Define the terminology used here and identify local variations. Numerical consistency cannot validate a reserve strategy or a navigation procedure. No current Cueva del Agua conditions were checked in this development task.

CDG's 2024 risk assessment appeared in search results but direct retrieval failed (404); it is not recorded as a fully checked source. An expert should especially review reserve-model assumptions, terminology, communication scenarios, common-mode failures and physiology before any status promotion.
