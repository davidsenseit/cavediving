## Audit

Audited navigation-related term occurrences across all 18 knowledge entries, 42 questions and existing learning pathways, then reviewed the affected passages. Standardized directional arrow wording and linked navigation lessons to the glossary. Retained ordinary words such as junction, line and marker where they describe broader categories rather than aliases for a specific subtype. Capability gap and communication gap are ordinary non-navigation uses.

Replaced generic navigational gap wording with break in continuity or separation where the end-to-end geometry was unspecified. Mainline is recognized as an alias; main line is the authored spelling. Navigation lessons now introduce T-junction and REM; pathways and assistant guidance require prerequisite teaching even when a learner chooses a quiz directly. No authored questions or answer keys were added or changed in meaning.
