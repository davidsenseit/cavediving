# Learning depth and course integration

Status: draft.

## Changes

Expanded causal explanations, explicit objectives, comparison cases and reasoning prompts across equipment, navigation, gas, physiology, planning, emergencies, environment and documentation. Added original conceptual diagrams for line followability and shared-power dependencies.

Added NAV-004, PLN-003, EMR-002 and PHY-001 with four linked questions. Integrated them into the existing eight modules, bringing the course to 25 core questions and 20 optional practice questions, plus the separate project appendix. Revised communication and emergency-capability alternatives and added reasoning follow-ups.

## Validation

The 20 existing automated checks pass for metadata, stable references, local links, terminology, course routing and educational arithmetic. Numerical examples and their arithmetic were not changed. Diagrams are conceptual, not site maps. All new and changed learning content remains draft.

These checks do not establish factual completeness, medical accuracy or practical proficiency. The new physiology explanations, emergency distinctions, readiness material and diagrams need substantive review before verified status. Equipment procedures and local navigation details require their applicable training context.
