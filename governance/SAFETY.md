# Safety and scope

This repository is a learning aid, not a certification body, dive supervisor, dive planner of record, or substitute for practical instruction.

Cave diving can result in serious injury or death. A correct-looking answer from an AI can still be wrong, incomplete or inappropriate for a particular site or dive team.

- Know and remain within actual training, experience and environmental limitations.
- Cross-check any real plan independently, including breathing gas, equipment, decompression, exit, and contingencies.
- Treat site conditions, lines and access information as potentially outdated until locally confirmed.
- Do not use a quiz score or verified knowledge item as evidence of practical diving competence.
- For unverified materials, the correct behavior is to flag uncertainty rather than invent missing procedures.
