# Treat a route discrepancy as unresolved

## Question

A pre-dive map review shows a connection, but the team’s current route information does not confirm a continuous line there. What conclusion is justified?

## Choices

A. The map guarantees that the line is present today.
B. A straight compass bearing replaces the missing physical reference.
C. A short distance makes line continuity irrelevant.
D. The map does not resolve present continuity; the route needs current verification within training.

## Answer and explanation

**D.** A survey records observations rather than guaranteeing present installations. A treats recorded information as current proof; B substitutes heading for continuity; C makes an unsupported distance exception. A map is useful evidence with limits.
