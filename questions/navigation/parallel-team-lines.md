# Identify the team route among nearby lines

## Question

Two temporary lines run close together in an entrance area. A teammate can see both but cannot identify which belongs to the team. What remains unresolved?

## Choices

A. Positive identification of the team’s continuous route to its verified exit.
B. Whether the brighter line is automatically the correct one.
C. Whether following either line guarantees the same exit.
D. Whether two visible lines eliminate the need for route identification.

## Answer and explanation

**A.** Visibility of several lines does not establish ownership or continuity to the team’s exit. B assigns meaning to appearance; C assumes destinations; D treats more lines as proof of clarity. Resolve the discrepancy within trained procedures rather than selecting by proximity.
