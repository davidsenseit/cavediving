# Line hazards and followability application

## Teaching notes
Objective: apply the linked lesson to the stated observations. Teach its distinctions before assessment using a different example.

## Question

A connected guideline passes through a narrow slot that the diver cannot fit through. What remains unresolved?

## Choices

A. Whether the line is physically continuous.
B. Whether the line path can be followed by the diver.
C. Whether the line has a recognizable colour.
D. Whether the line is temporary or permanent.

## Answer and explanation

**B.** The stated connection establishes continuity, but the slot prevents following the actual path. Colour and permanence do not solve clearance.

## Follow-up discussion

How would losing visibility change the significance of this placement?

## Tutor guidance

Look for recognition that tactile following cannot make an impassable path usable.
