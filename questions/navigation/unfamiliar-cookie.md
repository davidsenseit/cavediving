# Interpret a marker without assigning invented meaning

## Question

During a briefing, someone proposes treating every unfamiliar round cookie as a universal instruction to turn left. What is the problem with that rule?

## Choices

A. Cookies always identify the closest exit.
B. Round cookies have no intrinsic direction; their meaning depends on identification and agreed context.
C. Cookies always belong to the most recent team.
D. The rule becomes reliable if the cookie is a bright color.

## Answer and explanation

**B.** Shape alone gives a nondirectional cookie no left/right instruction. A invents an exit guarantee; C invents ownership; D assigns direction to color. Confirm local conventions and team meaning instead of importing a universal rule.
