# Interpret a dive log within its limits

## Question

A computer log shows a smooth depth trace for a completed dive. Someone claims this proves that the team maintained a continuous exit route and avoided disturbing sediment. What can the trace actually establish?

## Choices

A. A complete record of every line connection.
B. Proof that fin wash never reached the floor.
C. Recorded depth behavior, without proving route continuity or low-impact movement.
D. A certification of team competence.

## Answer and explanation

**C.** The trace measures depth behavior, not every navigation or movement action. A and B infer unrecorded observations; D turns a log into a competence assessment. Debriefing and practical observation address questions that depth data cannot settle.
