# Review passage access for the actual configuration

## Question

A passage was comfortable during a previous dive in a different equipment configuration. The team now proposes returning with bulkier equipment and additional tasks. What does the earlier visit establish?

## Choices

A. That all configurations will pass equally well.
B. That emergency access has automatically been demonstrated.
C. That a familiar site removes the need for a new assessment.
D. Past experience in the earlier configuration, without proving present suitability.

## Answer and explanation

**D.** The earlier visit is relevant but limited evidence. A generalizes passage fit; B assumes failure-response capability; C ignores changed equipment, tasks and conditions. Assess the actual configuration and training before committing to the proposed dive.
