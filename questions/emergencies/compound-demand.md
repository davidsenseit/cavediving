# Evaluate the combined failure case

## Question

A classroom contingency review considers reduced visibility and a shared-gas exit at the same time. The worksheet simply reuses the clear-water exit time without review. What is the strongest criticism?

## Choices

A. The combined event may increase delay and workload, so the original demand assumptions need reassessment.
B. Two difficulties always cancel each other’s effects.
C. A practiced response removes all extra gas demand.
D. Equipment redundancy makes exit duration irrelevant.

## Answer and explanation

**A.** A compound event can reduce capacity while increasing tasks. Its duration and demand cannot be assumed unchanged. B invents cancellation; C turns practice into a guarantee; D ignores breathing over time. The scenario does not prescribe an emergency rate or procedure.
