# Distinguishing emergency problems application

## Teaching notes
Objective: apply the linked lesson to the stated observations. Teach its distinctions before assessment using a different example.

## Question

A diver remains with the guideline, but a teammate is unaccounted for. Which description best identifies the known problem?

## Choices

A. Lost line, because team contact is uncertain.
B. Broken guideline, because the teammate is not visible.
C. A gas-supply failure, because separation increases demand.
D. Missing teammate, with guideline contact still maintained.

## Answer and explanation

**D.** The observation establishes separation, not loss or breakage of the guideline or a gas failure. Different problems may combine, but they must not be assumed.

## Follow-up discussion

What additional observations would change the problem description?

## Tutor guidance

Accept loss of line contact, a confirmed break or a breathing-supply problem; keep observations separate from assumptions.
