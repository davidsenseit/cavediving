# Calculate a stipulated sixths allocation

## Teaching notes

Teach GAS-006 first, including its guided calculation with different inputs. Assess units, method, arithmetic and explanation separately. Use the 1 bar reference, ideal-gas approximation and comparable temperature throughout. This is an authored classroom exercise, not a real dive plan.

## Question

A task explicitly restricts entry use to one sixth of starting inventory. A connected 24 L configuration starts at 216 bar. Calculate starting quantity, maximum entry use in bar and reference litres, nominal turn pressure and quantity remaining.

## Response format

Show your calculation with units, label the requested quantities and briefly explain the result. No answer choices are provided.

## Answer and explanation

Starting: 24 × 216 = **5,184 reference L**. Entry: 216 / 6 = **36 bar**, or **864 reference L**. Turn: 216 − 36 = **180 bar**. Remaining: 24 × 180 = **4,320 reference L**. This is five sixths remaining, not five sixths freely available for extra tasks.

## Follow-up discussion

Does this calculation establish which training level can use thirds instead?

## Tutor guidance

No. The restriction was supplied; privileges, configuration and conditions must be established separately.
Accept equivalent exact fractions and decimal notation. Where rounding is specified, assess the conservative direction as well as the value. Identify a conceptual error before offering another arithmetic attempt. Do not reveal a pending follow-up before the learner has answered the main calculation.
