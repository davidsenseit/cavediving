# Resolve a gas identity mismatch

## Question

Before entry, a cylinder’s analysis label and the planned gas setting disagree. The team has not established which information is correct. What is the sound decision?

## Choices

A. Use the planned setting because the plan was written first.
B. Use the label because a label cannot be mistaken.
C. Let the computer identify the cylinder contents automatically.
D. Resolve and verify the actual gas identity and appropriate settings before proceeding.

## Answer and explanation

**D.** A disagreement is unresolved information, not a choice of the more convenient number. A and B trust an unverified record; C assumes a capability that has not been established. Analysis, identification, labeling and device checks must agree under applicable training.
