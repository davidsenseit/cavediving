# Use separate cylinder readings

## Teaching notes

Teach GAS-004 first, including its guided calculation with different inputs. Assess units, method, arithmetic and explanation separately. Use the 1 bar reference, ideal-gas approximation and comparable temperature throughout. This is an authored classroom exercise, not a real dive plan.

## Question

Two independently used cylinders are each 11 L. Left reads 180 bar; right reads 150 bar. Estimate each reference quantity and the combined inventory. Explain why applying 180 bar to both would be wrong.

## Response format

Show your calculation with units, label the requested quantities and briefly explain the result. No answer choices are provided.

## Answer and explanation

Left: 11 × 180 = **1,980 reference L**. Right: 11 × 150 = **1,650 reference L**. Combined: 1,980 + 1,650 = **3,630 reference L**. Both readings must be respected. This combined inventory does not establish which gas is accessible after a particular failure.

## Follow-up discussion

What would you need to know before treating that sum as a donor supply?

## Tutor guidance

Configuration, failure and access/delivery assumptions. Adding quantities is not a demonstration that either diver can breathe all of them.
Accept equivalent exact fractions and decimal notation. Where rounding is specified, assess the conservative direction as well as the value. Identify a conceptual error before offering another arithmetic attempt. Do not reveal a pending follow-up before the learner has answered the main calculation.
