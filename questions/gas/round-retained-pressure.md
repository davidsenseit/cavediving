# Round a retained threshold

## Teaching notes

Teach GAS-007 first, including its guided calculation with different inputs. Assess units, method, arithmetic and explanation separately. Use the 1 bar reference, ideal-gas approximation and comparable temperature throughout. This is an authored classroom exercise, not a real dive plan.

## Question

A 24 L configuration starts at 225 bar. The exercise requires retaining 3,850 reference L and states that thresholds must be rounded upward to 5 bar steps. Find the exact pressure equivalent, rounded threshold, allowed entry drop and quantity, and retained quantity after rounding.

## Response format

Show your calculation with units, label the requested quantities and briefly explain the result. No answer choices are provided.

## Answer and explanation

Exact pressure is 3,850 / 24 = **160.4166… bar**. Round retention upward to **165 bar**. Allowed drop: 225 − 165 = **60 bar**; entry quantity **1,440 reference L**. Retained: 24 × 165 = **3,960 reference L**, exceeding the requirement by **110 reference L**. Rounding down to 160 bar would retain only 3,840 reference L.

## Follow-up discussion

Why is the direction different when rounding a maximum entry allowance?

## Tutor guidance

Allowable consumption rounds down; required retention rounds up. Reverse conversion reveals whether the requirement is met.
Accept equivalent exact fractions and decimal notation. Where rounding is specified, assess the conservative direction as well as the value. Identify a conceptual error before offering another arithmetic attempt. Do not reveal a pending follow-up before the learner has answered the main calculation.
