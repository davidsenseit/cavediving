# Two breathing records

## Teaching notes

- Learning objective: Compare normalized consumption across different cylinder volumes.
- Level: Comparison application
- Prerequisites: GAS-001 calculation steps; pressure-loss rate versus volume rate.

## Question

Two ideal-gas classroom records both show 1 bar/min pressure loss at constant 2 bar absolute. Record A uses 16 L total cylinder water volume; record B uses 24 L. Use comparable temperature and a 1 bar reference. Which pair of surface-normalized rates is correct?

## Choices

A. A: 16 L/min; B: 24 L/min.
B. A: 12 L/min; B: 8 L/min.
C. A: 8 L/min; B: 12 L/min.
D. A: 0.5 L/min; B: 0.5 L/min.

## Answer and explanation

**C.** For A, 16 × 1 ÷ 2 = 8 L/min; for B, 24 × 1 ÷ 2 = 12 L/min. A omits pressure normalization. B swaps the results, incorrectly assigning lower volume consumption to the larger configuration. D normalizes the pressure-loss rate without converting cylinder pressure loss to a gas volume. Equal bar/min is not equal L/min when water volumes differ.

## Follow-up discussion

At the same pressure and normalized rate, how would doubling cylinder water volume affect the bar/min loss?

### Tutor guidance

Expected: it halves. The same gas quantity is drawn from twice the water volume. Keep the distinction between cylinder pressure and ambient pressure explicit.
