# Compare pressure rates across configurations

## Question

Two classroom records show the same pressure loss: 1 bar/min at constant 2 bar absolute. One configuration has 16 L total cylinder water volume; the other has 24 L. Use ideal gas at comparable temperature and a 1 bar reference. Which interpretation is correct?

## Choices

A. Both records represent 1 L/min at the reference pressure.
B. The normalized rates are 8 and 12 L/min respectively.
C. The normalized rates are 16 and 24 L/min respectively.
D. Both divers have identical volume consumption because their pressure rates match.

## Answer and explanation

**B.** The respective reference-volume rates are 16 × 1 / 2 = 8 L/min and 24 × 1 / 2 = 12 L/min. A confuses pressure with volume; C omits ambient-pressure normalization; D ignores cylinder size. These measured rates do not establish emergency demand.
