# Compare quantities before comparing reserves

## Question

An ideal-gas inventory exercise compares 16 L total cylinder water volume at 230 bar with 24 L at 170 bar, at comparable temperature and a 1 bar reference. Ignore nonideal gas behavior. Which inventory statement is correct? No reserve or exit-demand model has yet been supplied.

## Choices

A. The 230 bar configuration must have more gas.
B. Both contain the same quantity because each uses two cylinders.
C. The larger inventory alone proves a sufficient donation reserve.
D. The inventories are approximately 3,680 L and 4,080 L; reserve adequacy is still unknown.

## Answer and explanation

**D.** Multiply water volume by pressure: 16 × 230 = 3,680 L and 24 × 170 = 4,080 L. A ignores volume, B treats cylinder count as capacity, and C substitutes inventory for a failure-and-demand assessment. Real high-pressure gas also departs from this idealization.
