# Check both equal donors

## Teaching notes

Teach GAS-008 first, including its guided calculation with different inputs. Assess units, method, arithmetic and explanation separately. Use the 1 bar reference, ideal-gas approximation and comparable temperature throughout. This is an authored classroom exercise, not a real dive plan.

## Question

A and B each have 24 L at 225 bar. Each exit demand is stipulated as one third of their own starting inventory, fixed during the exercise. Complete loss of either diver's gas is modeled, with all remaining donor gas deliverable. Calculate inventories, thirds turn pressures, combined demand and both donor surpluses. No additional margin is stipulated.

## Response format

Show your calculation with units, label the requested quantities and briefly explain the result. No answer choices are provided.

## Answer and explanation

Each inventory is **5,400 reference L**; each fixed demand is **1,800 reference L**. Thirds entry use is **75 bar**, giving **150 bar** thresholds. Combined demand is **3,600 reference L**. Each donor retains 24 × 150 = **3,600 reference L**. Surplus is **0 reference L** in either direction. Equality covers only the stated model, with no additional margin.

## Follow-up discussion

What does equal equipment fail to establish about breathing demand?

## Tutor guidance

It does not measure either diver's normal or stressed demand or route-dependent exit requirements.
Accept equivalent exact fractions and decimal notation. Where rounding is specified, assess the conservative direction as well as the value. Identify a conceptual error before offering another arithmetic attempt. Do not reveal a pending follow-up before the learner has answered the main calculation.
