# A fraction does not validate the failure model

## Question

A planning worksheet meets its chosen fractional entry limit. In a separate failure check, however, the modeled donor-accessible gas is below the modeled demand for both divers to exit. What conclusion follows?

## Choices

A. The fractional limit overrides the failure check.
B. Only the recipient needs to be included in exit demand.
C. The plan fails its stated failure check and needs revision before the dive.
D. Gas in an inaccessible cylinder can be added to remove the deficit.

## Answer and explanation

**C.** A percentage allocation cannot resolve an identified supply deficit. B removes the donor’s own breathing requirement; D counts unavailable gas. Revisit assumptions and limits with competent human review rather than treating a fraction as operational validation.
