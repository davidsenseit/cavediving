# Compare two stated allocations

## Teaching notes

Teach GAS-006 first, including its guided calculation with different inputs. Assess units, method, arithmetic and explanation separately. Use the 1 bar reference, ideal-gas approximation and comparable temperature throughout. This is an authored classroom exercise, not a real dive plan.

## Question

For 18 L at 240 bar, calculate entry allowance and retained quantity for two separate stipulated cases: sixths and thirds. Give both allowances and both turn pressures in bar, and the associated quantities in reference litres. Explain why neither fraction predicts distance.

## Response format

Show your calculation with units, label the requested quantities and briefly explain the result. No answer choices are provided.

## Answer and explanation

Starting quantity: **4,320 reference L**. Sixths: entry **40 bar = 720 reference L**; turn **200 bar**, retaining **3,600 reference L**. Thirds: entry **80 bar = 1,440 reference L**; turn **160 bar**, retaining **2,880 reference L**. Distance needs information about demand, depth, travel and conditions.

## Follow-up discussion

If a time limit is reached before the calculated pressure threshold, which governs?

## Tutor guidance

The first applicable limit governs. The comparison of fractions does not authorize an extension.
Accept equivalent exact fractions and decimal notation. Where rounding is specified, assess the conservative direction as well as the value. Identify a conceptual error before offering another arithmetic attempt. Do not reveal a pending follow-up before the learner has answered the main calculation.
