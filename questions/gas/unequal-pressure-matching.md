# Match unequal starts in equal capacities

## Teaching notes

Teach GAS-009 first, including its guided calculation with different inputs. Assess units, method, arithmetic and explanation separately. Use the 1 bar reference, ideal-gas approximation and comparable temperature throughout. This is an authored classroom exercise, not a real dive plan.

## Question

A and B each have 24 L. A starts at 225 bar; B at 195 bar. For this equal-capacity task, cap each entry use and stipulate each exit demand at one third of the lower starting inventory. All donor gas remaining at threshold is assumed deliverable. Calculate the common entry allowance, individual thresholds, combined demand and both donor surpluses.

## Response format

Show your calculation with units, label the requested quantities and briefly explain the result. No answer choices are provided.

## Answer and explanation

Lower inventory: 24 × 195 = **4,680 reference L**. Common entry allowance and individual exit demand: **1,560 reference L = 65 bar**. A turns at **160 bar**; B at **130 bar**. Combined demand: **3,120 reference L**. A retains **3,840 reference L**, surplus **720 reference L**; B retains **3,120 reference L**, surplus **0 reference L**.

## Interpretation check — ask separately after the calculation

B's computed donor surplus is zero under the stipulated model. Does this calculation alone establish that the team's emergency gas reserve is adequate for an actual cave dive? Name two missing operational inputs.

## Interpretation guidance

No. The stipulated demand and deliverability assumptions do not establish real exit duration, stressed breathing, delays, route constraints, or required residual reserve. A mathematically correct matching threshold is not operational approval. Evaluate this reasoning independently of the arithmetic result.

## Follow-up discussion

Why would assigning both divers the lower numerical threshold change the permitted entry use?

## Tutor guidance

A would use 95 bar instead of the stipulated 65 bar. Equal capacities allow a common drop here, but starts and final readings remain distinct.
Accept equivalent exact fractions and decimal notation. Where rounding is specified, assess the conservative direction as well as the value. Identify a conceptual error before offering another arithmetic attempt. Do not reveal a pending follow-up before the learner has answered the main calculation.
