# A survey is evidence with limits practice

## Question

A proposed survey adds work close to delicate sediment. Assess competence, permission, task loading and environmental effects before adding it to the dive.

Which conclusion can a historical map support by itself?

## Choices

A. That every opening is accessible today.
B. That sampling is authorized.
C. That the team can omit its route checks.
D. Only what the recorded survey actually documents, within its limits.

## Answer and explanation

**D.** A map is bounded evidence. The other conclusions require current access, permissions or route verification. No survey execution method is taught here.
