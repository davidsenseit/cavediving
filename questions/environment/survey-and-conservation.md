# A survey is evidence with limits practice

## Question

A historical survey depicts a passage where a team hopes to record additional observations.

What can that survey establish by itself?

## Choices

A. That the passage is accessible today.
B. That collecting samples is permitted.
C. That line checks are unnecessary.
D. The recorded geometry and observations within the survey's stated limits.

## Answer and explanation

**D.** Present access, permissions and route conditions require separate evidence. A survey does not authorize the proposed activity.
