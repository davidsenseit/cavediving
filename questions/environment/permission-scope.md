# Separate access permission from project permission

## Question

A landowner has permitted diving through an approved entrance. A team wants to add a survey station and collect a small specimen. No permission for either activity has been established. Which conclusion follows?

## Choices

A. Dive access alone does not establish permission for the additional activities.
B. Diving permission automatically includes specimen collection.
C. A small specimen is exempt from all access conditions.
D. A useful survey objective overrides conservation requirements.

## Answer and explanation

**A.** Permission has scope. Obtain the relevant authorization and assess conservation effects before adding activities. B broadens permission without evidence; C invents an exemption; D ranks the objective above access and protection requirements.
