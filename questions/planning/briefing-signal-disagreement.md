# Resolve communication disagreement before entry

## Question

In the briefing, two teammates demonstrate different meanings for the same tactile signal. Both assume they can explain it underwater if needed. What should happen before entry?

## Choices

A. Use whichever meaning the leader prefers without telling the team.
B. Resolve shared meaning and confirm communication within the team’s actual training.
C. Assume visibility will remain good enough to avoid the issue.
D. Add more tactile signals without checking the existing disagreement.

## Answer and explanation

**B.** A signal needs a shared, perceivable meaning. A preserves the mismatch; C depends on an unguaranteed condition; D adds complexity to an unresolved communication gap. A verbal agreement alone also does not demonstrate practical capability.
