# Team timeline review

## Teaching notes

- Learning objective: Distinguish a turnaround deadline from a maximum total dive duration.
- Level: Planning application
- Prerequisites: Turnaround time; total dive time; exit allowance; first applicable limit.

## Question

A fictional plan sets the latest turnaround at 25 minutes elapsed and the maximum total dive duration at 60 minutes elapsed. At minute 25, every diver is still above the agreed gas-turn threshold and the destination is three minutes farther in. Which action follows the plan?

## Choices

A. Continue for three minutes and deduct them from the planned exit allowance.
B. Continue until a gas threshold is reached because gas is the limiting resource.
C. Begin the return now because the turnaround deadline has been reached.
D. Continue until minute 30 because the return can use the other half of the hour.

## Answer and explanation

**C.** The 25-minute deadline ends penetration; the 60-minute limit includes the return. A spends time reserved by the existing plan on an optional destination. B lets one unused allowance override another reached limit. D invents a symmetric half-time rule that the plan does not contain. A turn deadline is a latest point, not a requirement to stay until then: another limit or a termination signal can require an earlier return.

## Follow-up discussion

How many minutes remain until the maximum duration at the planned turn, and why is that not permission for more penetration?

### Tutor guidance

Expected: 35 minutes. That interval includes the planned return and contingencies; its availability does not cancel the 25-minute turnaround deadline.
