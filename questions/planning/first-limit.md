# The first limit governs the team

## Question

The team’s maximum planned time has been reached, although every diver is still above the agreed gas-turn threshold and the destination is close. What governs the decision?

## Choices

A. The destination takes priority because it is close.
B. Only gas can end a cave dive.
C. Begin the agreed exit response because an applicable limit has been reached.
D. Extend the time automatically if the leader has the most gas.

## Answer and explanation

**C.** Limits apply together; gas remaining does not cancel a time boundary. A prioritizes the objective over a limit; B removes other constraints; D replaces the agreed plan with a unilateral extension.
