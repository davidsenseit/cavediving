# Readiness checks and emergency preparedness application

## Teaching notes
Objective: apply the linked lesson to the stated observations. Teach its distinctions before assessment using a different example.

## Question

All equipment is present and each regulator supplies gas during an individual check. The team has not checked donation access in the planned configuration. What evidence is still missing?

## Choices

A. Confirmation that the packing list contains both regulators.
B. A second inventory of cylinder accessories.
C. Evidence that the gas-sharing arrangement is accessible and usable by this team.
D. Agreement that both regulators are the same model.

## Answer and explanation

**C.** Individual operation does not establish access and team interaction. Recounting components or matching models does not resolve the stated gap.

## Follow-up discussion

What would a bubble check add, and what would it still not establish?

## Tutor guidance

Distinguish leak detection from donation access and demonstrated skill.
