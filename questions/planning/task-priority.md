# Remove an optional task when awareness suffers

## Question

While practicing a survey task under supervision, a diver repeatedly misses team communication and gas checks. Which change best addresses the immediate learning problem?

## Choices

A. Increase the survey target to encourage faster work.
B. Treat the missed checks as acceptable because the survey is useful.
C. Have the teammate assume all ongoing awareness for the diver.
D. Suspend the optional task and restore team awareness and control before reassessing workload.

## Answer and explanation

**D.** Optional objectives must fit available attention. A adds workload; B normalizes missed safety tasks; C removes rather than restores the diver’s awareness. Any in-water response remains subject to the instructor and trained team procedures.
