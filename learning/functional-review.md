# Equipment and log evidence exercise

Draft classroom worksheet, not an operational cave checklist.

| Evidence category | Record | Question to resolve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Packing | Item and intended function | Is the item present? |
| Function | Observed check, date and result | Does it operate and remain accessible in this configuration? |
| Dependencies | Shared power, gas or attachment | What can a single failure disable? |
| Device documentation | Model, installed firmware, mode, document revision | Does the guidance apply to this device state? |
| Log | Units, timestamps, measured versus inferred fields | What can and cannot be concluded? |
| Team brief | Route, constraints, communication and contingencies | Are assumptions shared and independently checked? |

Use fictional data, or obtain consent before publishing real logs. Mark unavailable inputs unknown. Do not invent reserve adequacy, navigation performance or medical conclusions from a depth graph. Ask the learner for one uncertainty and one way to verify it.
