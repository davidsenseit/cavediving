# Foundation pilot course

A ten-question draft course section for testing lesson clarity, reasoning and feedback. Use this sequence when the learner asks to try the pilot. It samples selected foundations; it is not a complete cave-diving syllabus.

## Teaching sequence

Read the linked lesson and check prerequisite terms in the [glossary](../GLOSSARY.md). Give a short explanation of the concept, then present only the question ID, Question and Choices. Keep file titles, Teaching notes, answers, follow-up prompts and tutor guidance hidden until the appropriate stage. Preserve the canonical English terms in Swedish teaching.

Use a different worked example from the assessment question. If teaching has just disclosed the assessment answer, explain the concept and use a different checked exercise labeled **AI-generated practice** before returning to the authored question later.

For stage 8, teach the demand relationship explicitly: combined normalized L/min × ambient/reference pressure ratio × minutes gives reference litres. If a rate already covers two divers, do not multiply it by two again. A delay adds another time segment, using the pressure and demand of that segment. Review this relationship before the error-analysis question, using different inputs.

## Feedback and discussion

Ask one question at a time. After the learner answers, explain the correct reasoning and address the misconception represented by their choice. A correct letter alone shows recognition; use the authored Follow-up discussion to explore understanding. Ask that prompt separately, before showing its Tutor guidance. Accept equivalent explanations rather than requiring particular wording.

For calculations, discuss inputs, units, formula and result. If the learner gives a correct method with an arithmetic slip, distinguish those in the feedback. If an alternative seems defensible because the wording is unclear, examine the ambiguity before assessing the learner.

Within the current conversation, distinguish correct recognition, explained reasoning and a concept needing another example. At the end, revisit the concepts that remained uncertain with a materially different checked exercise. Keep any comments on question clarity separate from the assessment of the learner’s knowledge. Practical competence and real-dive planning require their own checks.
