# Navigation foundation

Before assessment, introduce unfamiliar terms using the [glossary](../GLOSSARY.md) and the linked knowledge entries. Keep canonical English terms in all teaching languages; if teaching a term reveals a pending answer, choose another question afterward.

For the full navigation module, follow Module 3 of the [course](core-pathway.md). This focused exercise revisits NAV-001; return to the course afterward, preserving completed question IDs.

1. Read [NAV-001](../knowledge/navigation/guideline-continuity.md).
2. Ask the learner to explain the value of a continuous, positively identified guideline in their own words.
3. Present [NAV-Q001](../questions/navigation/continuity.md) without the answer.
4. After their response, explain and discuss misconceptions.
5. Ask NAV-Q001’s authored follow-up separately and use its Tutor guidance to assess the explanation. If uncertainty remains, offer a checked **AI-generated practice** question with a materially different setting.
6. Invite the learner to request a deeper explanation or select another topic.

No pass/fail classification is needed.
